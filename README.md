On the EEA schedule, the entry tier for an account that has opened a derivatives (X-Perps) account is **0.0800% maker and 0.1000% taker**. VIP1 moves to 0.0675% / 0.0800%, and the top tier listed is -0.0075% maker / 0.0175% taker, where the negative maker figure is a rebate paid to you rather than a charge. OKX states that fees vary by region and points users to a region-specific fee page, so treat those numbers as a reference tier rather than a quote for your own account.

The fee is calculated as the rate multiplied by the amount of crypto bought when the order fills. A market order that fills immediately against the book pays the taker rate; a limit order that sits on the book until someone takes it pays the maker rate.

For a first purchase, one OKX detail matters more than the tier table. **Buy & Sell and Convert quote a single price with a spread built into it**, and that spread replaces the separate maker and taker fee. Comparing a 0.1000% taker rate against the price you get from a one-click buy compares two different things. If you want the order-book rate, place a limit order. If you want the simple flow, your cost is embedded in the price shown on screen.

Referral codes don't change any of this. OKX's help documentation says maker and taker rates come from the fee tier your account sits on, so signing up with a code doesn't by itself move an account to a different rate, and any rebate from a programme is handled under that programme's terms rather than by editing the published schedule. That is worth knowing before you assume a signup link lowers your trading cost.

## How to buy PEPE on Coinbase, step by step

Coinbase lists seven steps for buying PEPE on its centralized exchange:

1. Download the app, sign up, and have a valid ID ready. Proof of address may also be requested.
2. Add a payment method in the payment method section: bank account, debit card, or wire transfer.
3. Tap Buy & Sell, then the (+) Buy button.
4. Search for Pepe in the asset list and select it.
5. Enter the amount in your local currency. The minimum is 1, and the app converts it into PEPE.
6. Select "Review order," check the details, then hit "Buy now."
7. A confirmation screen means the order went through.

The payment rules on this page are stricter than the ones people assume. Credit cards **cannot** be used to purchase PEPE. PayPal works for buying PEPE or adding US dollars only for US customers; everyone else can use PayPal only to sell or cash out, and availability depends on region. Digital gift cards bought from online merchants work for PEPE purchases in the US, but cannot be redeemed in Hawaii.

On cost, Coinbase says it may charge a fee calculated when you place the order, influenced by your location, the payment method, order size, and market conditions such as volatility and liquidity. Fees for similar transactions can differ. The trade preview screen is where the number appears, which is the only place it can be read before you commit.

## How to buy PEPE on MetaMask, step by step

MetaMask is the self-custody route: PEPE lands in a wallet you control, with no exchange account holding it for you. The mobile flow is nine steps.

1. Open MetaMask on your phone, or install the mobile app first.
2. Tap Buy on the home screen.
3. Select Buy on the next screen.
4. Choose your region and currency.
5. Select Pepe and the network.
6. Enter the amount you want to buy.
7. Choose a payment method: credit card, debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal. Tapping a provider name lets you compare quotes.
8. Complete the purchase through the payment flow.
9. PEPE arrives in your wallet once the transaction is confirmed.

Two things make this route different from an exchange. Payment options come from multiple providers, so the price you pay can differ between them on the same order, and MetaMask lets you compare before choosing. And the networks supported include Ethereum, Solana and Polygon, which means you should pick the network deliberately rather than accepting a default. Payment method availability varies by region, and MetaMask does not publish a country-by-country list.

## The same-name trap when you search "Pepe"

Search results for Pepe include an asset literally called "Pepe is for everyone," priced at $0.0000004531 with a total supply of **1,000,000,000** and a market cap of about **$453.13**. It trades only through decentralized exchanges on Ethereum, Base or Solana, requires USDC to swap into, and carries a 1% swap fee plus ETH network gas on that route.

Real PEPE has a supply of 413,772,350,098,130 and a market cap of $2.01B. The two differ by roughly six orders of magnitude in supply and seven in market cap. Before confirming any order, check the supply figure and the market cap on the asset page. If the page shows a supply in the billions rather than the hundreds of trillions, you are looking at a different token with the same ticker, and the 1% swap fee quoted there applies to that token, not to a PEPE spot purchase on an exchange.
